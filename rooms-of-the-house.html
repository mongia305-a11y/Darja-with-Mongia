Language and culture

Inside a Tunisian dar

These short notes show how some house words appear in everyday Tunisian life.

🚪

The blue door — الباب الأزرق

Blue-and-white doors are a well-known feature of Sidi Bou Said. In Tunisian Arabic, el-bab lazra9 means “the blue door.”

🫖

Welcoming a guest — تفضّل أقعد

When welcoming someone into the living room, you can say tfadhel, o93od — “please, sit down.”

🫒

The courtyard — الحوش

In many traditional Tunisian houses, el-7ouch is the central courtyard and an important family space.
