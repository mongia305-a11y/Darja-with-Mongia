How to Say “I Don’t Understand” in Tunisian Arabic

Learn how to ask for repetition, request slower speech, and keep a conversation going in Tunisian Arabic.

2. Reusable chunks
3. Quick react
4. Live role-play
Step 01 & 02

The Mini-Dialogue

8 dialogue lines

Listen first: Listen before reading. Notice the sounds and rhythm, then open the transcript when you are ready.

Playing audio
Nour 3aslema Anna, n7eb nes2lek 7aja.
عسلامة أنَا، نحبّ نسألك حاجة.
Hi Anna, I want to ask you something.
Anna 3aslema! Tfaddhel, es2el.
عسلامة! تفضّل، اسأل.
Hi! Go ahead, ask.
Nour Win t7eb temchi el youm?
وين تحبّ تمشي اليوم؟
Where do you want to go today?
Anna Same7ni, ma fhemtch. Tnajjem t3awed?
سامحني، ما فهِمتش. تنجّم تعاود؟
Sorry, I didn’t understand. Can you repeat?
Nour Akid. 9otlek: win t7eb temchi el youm?
أكيد. قلتلك: وين تحبّ تمشي اليوم؟
Of course. I said: where do you want to go today?
Anna Tnajjem ta7ki b-chwaya?
تنجّم تحكي بشويّة؟
Can you speak slowly?
Nour Ey, behi. Win t7eb temchi el youm?
إي، باهي. وين تحبّ تمشي اليوم؟
Yes, okay. Where do you want to go today?
Anna Ah, tawwa fhemt! N7eb nemchi lel medina el 3arbi.
آه، توّا فهمت! نحبّ نمشي للمدينة العربي.
Ah, now I understand! I want to go to the old city.

Cultural Insight

المدينة العربي · El medina el 3arbi

In Tunisian conversation, المدينة العربي refers to the medina: the historic old part of town. Picture narrow lanes, traditional houses, small shops and souks. Here, the phrase means “the old town,” rather than a separate city. Use نحبّ نمشي للمدينة العربي (N7eb nemchi lel medina el 3arbi) to say “I want to go to the medina.”
