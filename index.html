Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know Arabic?

No. Our courses are designed for complete beginners as well as learners with some Arabic background.

Is this Tunisian Darja or Standard Arabic?

Darja with Mongia focuses on Tunisian Arabic (Darja), the language spoken in everyday life in Tunisia.

Can I take private lessons?

Yes! Private one-on-one lessons are available through the Private Lessons section.

About Darja with Mongia

Hello, I'm Mongia, a native Tunisian Arabic tutor with several years of online teaching experience. I help learners speak Tunisian Arabic with confidence through structured lessons, practical conversations, and cultural insights. Whether you're learning for family, travel, relationships, or personal interest, my goal is to help you communicate naturally and understand Tunisian culture along the way. Darja with Mongia was created to make Tunisian Arabic accessible through clear, step-by-step learning.
