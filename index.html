    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do I need to know Arabic?

    No. The free beginner lessons include Latin spelling, English meanings and audio, so you can start even if you cannot read Arabic.

    Is this Tunisian Arabic or Standard Arabic?

    Darja with Mongia focuses on Tunisian Arabic (Darja), the language spoken in everyday life in Tunisia.

    Can I take private lessons?

    Yes. You can view Mongia’s private lessons on Preply for availability and booking.

    What does “Darja” mean?

    “Darja” means “dialect.” Tunisian Darja is the variety of Arabic that Tunisians use in everyday conversations with family, friends and people in their community.

    Do I need to speak English?

    A basic level of English is enough. I use simple English to explain vocabulary, grammar and activities during lessons. You do not need to speak English fluently.

    Are the podcast and learning resources free?

    Yes. The podcast episodes, transcriptions and selected learning resources on this website are available for free.
