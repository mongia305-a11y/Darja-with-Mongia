Cultural Insight

Tunisian Everyday Greetings

In Tunisia, عسلامة (3asslema) is universally loved and heard dozens of times daily in shops, streets, and homes. You may also frequently hear السلام عليكم (es-salamu 3alaykom), which is more formal and traditional. Placing your right hand gently on your chest after greeting is a warm gesture of heartfelt respect.
